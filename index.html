tmt-loader

Plays incremental games built on The Modding Tree in your browser, each on the engine version it was written for — including games whose own page no longer works. Progress saves automatically, in this browser only and separately for each game.

The games are listed, ranked and described on the TMT fork census. Its ▶ loader and ▶ mobile links open a game here.

Playing on a phone, or want extra tools? Options you can turn on
Mobile layout
One column with large buttons: the tree first, then the layer you open at full width. For phones.
Nav bar
A bar along the bottom of the screen, with a Layers view that lists every layer as a card with its reset button and what you can buy. Useful on a desktop too; the mobile layout always includes it.
Automation tools
An extra AU tab that can reset and buy things for you. Every feature starts off; you choose what to turn on. Nothing in the game itself is changed.

To turn one on, open a game, open its options (the game's settings button) and use the tmt-loader buttons at the bottom. The page reloads, and the choice is remembered in this browser for every game.
