The Modding Tree games

Incremental games built on The Modding Tree, each running on the engine version it was written for. Pick one below to play it in this browser — nothing to install. Not sure where to start? Prestige Tree Rewritten is the classic.

Your progress saves automatically, in this browser only and separately for each game. To keep a copy or move it to another device, use the game's own Export and Import buttons in its options.

Playing on a phone, or want extra tools? Options you can turn on
Mobile layout
One column with large buttons: the tree first, then the layer you open at full width. For phones.
Nav bar
A bar along the bottom of the screen, with a Layers view that lists every layer as a card with its reset button and what you can buy. Useful on a desktop too; the mobile layout always includes it.
Automation tools
An extra AU tab that can reset and buy things for you. Every feature starts off; you choose what to turn on. Nothing in the game itself is changed.

To turn one on, open any game, open its options (the game's settings button) and use the tmt-loader buttons at the bottom. The page reloads, and the choice is remembered in this browser for every game.
